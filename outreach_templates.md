# Outreach Templates & BANT Qualification Guide

Used by the sales team while working leads through the pipeline for **PulseCRM**
(sales/event-analytics SaaS sold to mid-market retail, D2C, and logistics companies).

---

## BANT Qualification Framework

Every lead is scored 1-5 on each dimension during the "Contacted" and "Qualified"
stages. A lead needs a combined score of **15+ out of 20** to be formally marked
"Qualified" and move to Proposal stage — the pipeline data confirms this cutoff is
predictive (see README insights).

| Dimension | 1 (Poor fit) | 3 (Moderate) | 5 (Strong fit) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Budget** | No allocated budget, exploratory only | Budget exists but needs approval | Budget confirmed and approved |
| **Authority** | Contact is an individual contributor | Contact influences the decision | Contact is the final decision-maker |
| **Need** | Vague interest, no clear pain point | Acknowledged problem, not urgent | Clear, quantified pain point |
| **Timeline** | No timeline / "just researching" | Buying within 2 quarters | Buying within 30-60 days |

### Discovery call questions (used to score BANT)

- **Budget:** "Has budget been allocated for solving this, or would this need new sign-off?"
- **Authority:** "Who else is typically involved when your team adopts a new tool like this?"
- **Need:** "Walk me through how this problem affects your team today — what's the cost of not fixing it?"
- **Timeline:** "Is there a specific event or deadline driving the timeline on this?"

## Objection Handling — Common Lost Reasons

| Objection | Response angle |
|---|---|
| "Budget too high" | Offer phased rollout (start with core team, expand later) or annual pre-pay discount |
| "Chose a competitor" | Ask for feedback on the decision; keep door open for 6-month check-in |
| "No decision / went dark" | One final "close the loop" email — low-pressure, easy to ignore or re-engage |
| "Not enough authority" | Ask contact to help get the right stakeholder into a short call |
| "Bad timing" | Set a calendar reminder to re-approach in the timeframe they mentioned |

## Cold Call Opening Script

"Hi [First Name], this is [Rep Name] from PulseCRM — I know this is out of the blue,
so I'll be quick. We help [industry] companies get live visibility into their sales
pipeline instead of pulling numbers manually every week. Is that something on your
radar at all, or is this a bad time?"

*(If yes → move to discovery questions. If no → ask "Who on your team would be the
right person to ask?" and get a referral instead of ending the call.)*
